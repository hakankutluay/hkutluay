Listen. Measure. Understand.

Watchmaker-grade numbers from the phone in your pocket.

  • Live measurement

    Rate in seconds per day, beat error in milliseconds, amplitude in degrees and a stability reading — with automatic beat-rate detection from 12,000 to 36,000 bph.

  • Classic trace

    The two-line paper-tape trace from the bench: slope shows rate, gap shows beat error. A signal-quality meter tells you when to trust the numbers.

  • Accuracy tracking

    Tap along with the seconds hand every few days to learn how many seconds a day your watch really gains or loses on the wrist. No microphone needed.

  • Positional test

    A guided run through the six regulation positions. Chronolyze computes the positional variation (Δ) a watchmaker regulates against, and explains it.

  • Magnetization check

    A suddenly fast watch is usually magnetized. Your iPhone’s magnetometer checks in seconds, before you pay for a service you don’t need.

  • Watch collection

    Per-watch lift angle and beat rate, full measurement history, saved positional runs and re-viewable traces for every session.

  • 104 movements built in

    Seiko, ETA, Sellita, Miyota, Rolex, Omega, Grand Seiko, Tudor and more, each with the right lift angle and beat rate already set.

  • Plain-language verdicts

    Every finished measurement ends with a clear verdict and a report card you can share.

What you need

A quiet room and a mechanical watch, hand-wound or automatic. Rest the case against the bottom edge of your iPhone, where the microphone is. Quartz watches don’t tick the same way — Chronolyze recognises one and says so rather than reporting nonsense.

Chronolyze is a reference tool, not professional instrumentation. Readings are approximate and depend on ambient noise, positioning and your device. For a definitive diagnosis or regulation, consult a qualified watchmaker.
