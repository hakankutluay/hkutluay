Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I add a reminder? Tap the + button on the Reminders tab, enter a name, choose an emoji, and set a repeat interval.
  • How do I pause a reminder? Open the reminder's detail view and tap Pause. Paused reminders appear in the Paused tab and won't send notifications.
  • How do I change the repeat interval? Open the reminder's detail view, tap Edit, and adjust the schedule.
  • Why didn't I get a notification? Make sure Reelo has notification permission in Settings → Reelo → Notifications.
  • How do I delete a reminder? Open the reminder's detail view and tap Delete.
