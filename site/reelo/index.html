Smart recurring reminders

Reminders that restart when you do.

Contact lenses every 30 days. Water the plant every 3 days. Car service every 6 months. Set it once — Reelo resets each reminder when you mark it done.

Free · iPhone & iPad · iOS 26 or later

Built for life on repeat

Not tasks. Recurring life tasks.

  • Counts from completion

    Mark a reminder done and the next one counts from today, not from the date you missed.

  • Flexible scheduling

    Repeat every N days, weeks, months or years, or set a one-time reminder that clears itself when done. Choose the exact time to be notified.

  • Follow-up nudges

    Get a heads-up days before something is due, and optional nudges at 1, 2, 4 and 8 hours if you miss it.

  • Stay organized

    Reminders grouped into Overdue, Today, This Week and Later, with full-text search across titles and notes.

  • Pause anytime

    Taking a break? Pause any reminder without losing it, and resume it from the Paused tab when you’re ready.

  • Calendar view

    A month-by-month calendar shows what’s coming. Tap any day to see what’s due.

  • Widgets

    Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with live countdowns, so you know what’s next at a glance.

  • iCloud sync

    Your reminders follow you to every device through your own iCloud account.

Set it once. Never forget again.
